Travel Track

Group Safety Radar

>_ BRIEFING: Travel Track is an off-grid group tracking and safety radar mobile app for hikers and adventurers, connecting nearby devices via Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth Low Energy without cellular coverage.

// KEY SAFETY SYSTEMS

  • Off-Grid Radar: P2P Wi-Fi Direct peer mesh network.
  • Directional Compass: Live bearing and distance telemetry.
  • One-Touch SOS: High-priority distress broadcast alert.
  • Offline Maps: Pre-cached topographic GPS trail routes.
