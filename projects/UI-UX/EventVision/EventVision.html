EventVision

Ticketing & Event Portal

>_ BRIEFING: EventVision is an event discovery, ticket booking, and virtual seat selection platform UI, providing concert-goers with 3D stage view previews and digital passes.

// KEY EVENT SYSTEMS

  • Event Feed: Category cards with date, venue & tickets.
  • Tier Selection: VIP, early bird, and general passes.
  • Digital Passes: QR code access and mobile wallet sync.
  • Organizer Hub: Real-time ticket sales and check-ins.
