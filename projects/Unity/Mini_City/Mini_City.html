Mini City

Urban Exploration

>_ BRIEFING: Mini City is an open-world low-poly vehicle driving and city navigation simulation in Unity 3D. Cruise through bustling street networks, reference real-time mini-maps, and inspect urban landmarks.

// KEY EXPLORATION SYSTEMS

  • Vehicle Physics: Steering curves and drift damping.
  • Modular City: Low-poly road grids and intersections.
  • Dual Navigation: Top-down map and live mini-map radar.
  • Camera System: Smooth chase camera with lerping.
