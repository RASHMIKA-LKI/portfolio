My Journey

GAME DEVELOPER

// ISHAN RASHMIKA • SPECIALIZED IN IMMERSIVE GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS

I’m Ishan Rashmika, a Unity Developer and 3D Artist studying for a Bachelor of ICT (Honours) at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura. I build 2D and 3D games, interactive simulations, and models using Unity, C#, and Blender. My projects include vehicle controls in Mini City, shopping interactions in Store Simulation, and planets moving around the Sun in Solar System.

My work also includes shooting games such as Farm Shoot and 2D Space Shooter, along with spacecraft and environment modeling in Blender. Through university assignments, personal projects, and online courses, I continue to improve my programming and design skills. I enjoy solving problems, exploring ideas, and creating experiences that people can play and interact with.
